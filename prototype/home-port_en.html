About the Ship

MSC Euribia is the newest masterpiece of MSC Cruises' Meraviglia Plus Class, named after Eurybia, the sea goddess of ancient Greek mythology. The ship is renowned for industry-leading environmental technology and artistic flair, with an iconic "ocean manifesto" mural painted across her hull symbolizing a commitment to a sustainable ocean. On board you'll find a 112-meter LED-domed indoor promenade, five distinctive water-sports parks, and diverse dining of Michelin-star caliber — a floating palace of art that pairs refined luxury with environmental care.

Cancellation Policy

To cancel an order, please be sure to contact customer service directly to protect your rights.

If the customer terminates the main contract (hereinafter "cancellation") and cancels the cruise itinerary, the operator may charge the following cancellation fees as damages:

■ Cancellation Penalties, Cruises Shorter Than 15 Nights
  • From order confirmation to 70 or more days before departure (incl. day 60): TWD 5,000 per person
  • 69 to 60 working days before departure: TWD 6,000 per person
  • 59 to 50 working days before departure: TWD 8,000 per person
  • 49 to 30 working days before departure: 30% of the full cruise fare
  • 29 to 15 days before departure: 50% of the full cruise fare
  • Within 14 working days of departure: 100% of the full cruise fare
■ Cancellation Penalties, Cruises of 15 Nights or Longer
  • From order confirmation to 75 or more days before departure (incl. day 75): TWD 30,000 per person.
  • 74 to 60 working days before departure: 30% of the full cruise fare.
  • 59 to 45 working days before departure: 50% of the full cruise fare.
  • Within 44 working days of departure: 100% of the full cruise fare.

※ If the customer cancels after departure or leaves the cruise mid-voyage, all amounts paid (including port taxes and other related fees) are retained as damages and are non-refundable.

※ If the customer cancels due to a visa refusal, the standard cancellation policy and penalties above still apply; the customer bears the associated risk.

※ For individual name-change requests submitted 14 or more working days before departure, an administrative fee of TWD 3,600 applies per change.

※ If cruise tickets have already been issued and must be reissued due to changes to the customer's information, the customer must pay a TWD 3,600 "reissue fee" within one working day. If not paid on time, the operator or the cruise line may decline the request and bears no responsibility for any delay.

※ Cancellation fees for special voyages are not subject to the standard rates above; the operator's written notice or the cruise line's published rules prevail.

※ All day counts above are based on working days. If a cutoff date falls on a national holiday or weekend, it moves up to the preceding working day.
