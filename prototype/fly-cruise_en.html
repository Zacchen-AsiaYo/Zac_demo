About the Ship

■ Cruise Line: Norwegian Cruise Line
■ Ship Name: 【 NCL Encore 】 Alaska 8-Day Cruise: Glacier Bay, Skagway, Juneau & Ketchikan

Relive unforgettable moments aboard the newly refreshed NCL Encore — a ship built for "let's do that again!" Race the Encore Speedway, unwind in the sauna or salt room of the Thermal Suite, and raise a glass to panoramic views in the Observation Lounge.

Transportation

■ Transportation: Ground Transfers
  • How to book: Register for an NCL account, link your booking number, then go to Check-In in your booking management, enter your flight details, and reserve this paid service.
  • This service is available on embarkation day only. It is typically a shared shuttle bus with other guests, so some waiting may be required.
  • Airport pickup: an NCL representative will meet you at baggage claim (domestic flights) or after you clear customs (international flights).
  • Airport drop-off: onboard staff will advise you of the bus departure time and location.

Cancellation Policy

  • If the customer terminates the main contract (hereinafter "cancellation") and cancels the cruise itinerary, the operator may charge the following cancellation fees as damages:
    • ★ (a) Cancellations 90 or more days before departure (after order confirmation): free of charge.
    • ★ (b) Cancellations 89-75 working days before departure: 20% of the full cruise fare.
    • ★ (c) Cancellations 74-50 working days before departure: 25% of the full cruise fare.
    • ★ (d) Cancellations 49-29 working days before departure: 50% of the full cruise fare.
    • ★ (e) Cancellations 28-15 working days before departure: 75% of the full cruise fare.
    • ★ (e) Cancellations 14 working days or fewer before departure (incl. day 14): 100% of the full cruise fare.
  • Other necessary costs arising from a cancellation — including but not limited to single supplements or shore excursion cancellation costs — are borne by the canceling traveler
  • Per the cruise line's rules, bookings must use the correct name on a valid passport; once the booking is completed, no name changes or traveler information changes are accepted for any stateroom.
  • Changing essential booking details (traveler information, departure date, cabin category, etc.) is treated as a cancellation and rebooking. See the previous item for applicable cancellation fees.
  • Any booking change or cancellation must be formally submitted in writing or by email, stating the date and details, to be valid; phone or verbal notice is not accepted. Around domestic or foreign public holidays, please notify us in advance so the cruise line's cancellation date calculations can be met.
